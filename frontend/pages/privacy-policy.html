Data Protection & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

TimeCraft collects only the personal information required to fulfil insured timepiece orders, verify warranty ownership and provide horological concierge support.

Credentials & Tokens: Passwords are stored exclusively as BCrypt hashes. Refresh tokens, email verification tokens and password reset tokens are stored as SHA-256 digests and never in plain text.

Payments: Online payments are processed via Razorpay. TimeCraft never stores card numbers, CVV codes or UPI PINs on its servers.
