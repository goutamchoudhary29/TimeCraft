Frequently Asked Questions

Are all watches sold on TimeCraft brand new and authentic?

Yes. Every timepiece is brand new, serial-registered, accompanied by its official maison warranty card and inspected by our in-house horologists prior to dispatch.

How does stock reservation work during checkout?

When you initiate checkout, the exact variant quantity is locked in our inventory ledger for 15 minutes while you complete Razorpay payment. If payment is not completed within the window, the reservation releases automatically.

What is your return and refund policy?

Unworn timepieces with intact factory seals, presentation box and warranty papers may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Once inspected and received at our vault, refunds are processed back to the original Razorpay payment method.

Who can submit a product review?

Only verified purchasers who have a DELIVERED order containing that timepiece can submit a review, ensuring every rating on TimeCraft reflects genuine ownership.
